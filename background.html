01

How the brands were chosen

The index covers thirty of the world's most significant automakers, drawn from three distinct pillars of the industry so the list reflects the full spectrum of who builds fast cars.

  • Dedicated hypercar houses — companies that exist to chase speed above all else: Koenigsegg, Bugatti, SSC, Hennessey, Pagani, Rimac and Pininfarina.
  • Performance legacy giants — marques with decades of racing and engineering pedigree: Ferrari, Porsche, McLaren, Aston Martin, Lamborghini, Lotus, Mercedes-AMG, Audi, BMW and Jaguar.
  • Global mainstream builders — the volume manufacturers that produced an exceptional fast car: Toyota, Honda, Ford, Chevrolet, Dodge, Nissan, Hyundai, Kia, Tesla, Lucid, Bentley, Rolls-Royce and Polestar.

A brand qualifies through global reach, racing heritage or performance influence. The result is a balanced grid — never a popularity contest, never a random draw.

02

How the cars were chosen

Two hard rules govern every entry. First, one car per brand — a guarantee that no single manufacturer can dominate the field. Second, the car must be the fastest road-legal production model that brand actually builds and sells today.

  • No concepts. A car must be real, certified and available to the public.
  • No prototypes or one-offs. If a brand built exactly one, it stays off the list.
  • No tuners. Every entry is an official factory model, not an aftermarket build.

If a brand releases a faster model, that new car replaces the old one on the index. A car cannot enter here on reputation alone — it must be the quickest the manufacturer genuinely produces.

03

Method and verification

The field is ranked purely by top speed in miles per hour, fastest to least fast, using figures that trace back to a published claim or a documented, verified run. Where two cars top out at the same number, the order is decided by objective ties.

  • First tie-break: 0-60 mph — the quicker launch wins.
  • Second tie-break: peak power — the stronger engine wins.
  • Figures follow the manufacturer's factory specification for the road-legal production version.

Every number on the leaderboard is deliberate and accountable. The champs here were chosen by the data — never by chance.

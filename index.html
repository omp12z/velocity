BACKGROUND

How the 30 brands and their cars were selected — every entry is deliberate, chosen by data and reach, never at random.

01

How the brands were chosen

Thirty of the world's most significant automakers — dedicated hypercar houses (Koenigsegg, Pagani, Bugatti, Hennessey), electric pioneers (Tesla, Rimac, Lucid), and global giants (Toyota, Ford, Volkswagen Group's Audi, Porsche, Lamborghini and Bentley). Each earns a place through global presence, racing heritage or performance engineering.

02

How the cars were chosen

One car per brand — the fastest road-legal production car each maker has certified. No concepts, no prototypes, no one-offs. If a brand sells a faster model today, that is the model listed. A car cannot enter this index on reputation alone; it must be the quickest the manufacturer actually builds.

03

Method and verification

Ranking is by top speed in miles per hour, highest to lowest, drawn from official figures and documented records. Where two cars top out identically, 0-60 mph and power decide the order. Every listing is traceable to a published claim or a verified run — chosen by the numbers, not by chance.
